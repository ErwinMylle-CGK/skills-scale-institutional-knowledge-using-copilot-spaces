## OctoAcme project management process overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative lifecycle that keeps work aligned from idea to delivery. The process begins with initiation, where teams confirm the problem, identify stakeholders, define success measures, and decide whether to proceed. Once approved, the team moves into planning to break the work into a backlog, estimates, milestones, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.

During execution, the team delivers small increments through the project board and pull request workflow, while tracking progress, managing blockers, and validating quality with automated checks and manual QA. Risk and communication practices are maintained throughout the lifecycle using a risk register, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths for issues that need additional support.

When work is ready to ship, the release and deployment guide standardizes pre-release checks, verification, observability, and rollback planning. After each sprint, release, or major milestone, the team runs a retrospective to capture lessons learned and convert them into action items that improve future delivery.

## Lifecycle at a glance

1. Initiation — validate the need, align stakeholders, and confirm the go/no-go decision.
2. Planning — define scope, milestones, backlog, roles, risks, and Definition of Done.
3. Execution — build, test, review, and track delivery progress in small increments.
4. Release — verify readiness, deploy safely, and communicate changes to stakeholders.
5. Close and improve — capture learning, document outcomes, and carry forward action items.
